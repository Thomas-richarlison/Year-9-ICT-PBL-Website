FAQ

Ten questions people actually ask about fire in Myanmar — with straight answers.

10 questions

Questions people ask about fire

Tap a question to open its answer. Every answer is based on the same sources as the rest of this site.

01 Why is fire Myanmar's most frequent disaster?

Fires are recorded more often than floods or storms — usually 900 to 2,100 outbreaks every year. The long dry season, flammable bamboo and timber housing, and small everyday mistakes around cooking and wiring all combine to make fires the most common hazard.

02 What number should I call when I see a fire?

Call 191 for the fire service and rescue — it is free and works 24 hours. Ambulance is 192, police is 199, and the Myanmar Red Cross is 01-295-133. Save all four in your phone before you ever need them.

03 What causes most house fires in Myanmar?

Negligence with open flames — cooking left unattended, candles during power cuts — plus faulty electrical wiring, discarded cigarettes and agricultural burning. Fire officers say most of these are entirely preventable.

04 When is fire risk the highest?

During the dry season, roughly November to May, and especially March to May. The heat, wind and dry vegetation turn wood, bamboo and thatch into fuel, so a single spark can take a whole lane of houses.

05 A fire starts in my home — what do I do first?

Shout to warn everyone, call 191, then leave immediately by the nearest safe exit and stay out. Never go back for belongings or animals. If your clothes catch fire: stop, drop to the ground and roll.

06 How do I escape through smoke?

Stay low and crawl — the cleanest air stays near the floor. Cover your nose and mouth with a damp cloth. Feel a door with the back of your hand before opening it; if it is hot, use another way out.

07 What should a family emergency go-bag contain?

Drinking water, a torch with spare batteries, a first-aid kit, a whistle, copies of important documents, some cash and any medicine your family needs. Keep it where everyone can grab it in seconds.

08 How can my neighbourhood prepare before the dry season?

Hold a fire drill and agree a meeting point, keep lanes and exits clear, check electrical connections, organise a volunteer fire watch with the township fire station, and make sure every household knows the 191 number.

09 Which parts of Myanmar report the most fires?

Between January and October 2025, Yangon Region recorded 180 fires, Mandalay 132 and Shan State 101. Five regions — Yangon, Mandalay, Ayeyarwady, Sagaing and Bago — account for about 63% of all cases.

10 Where can I learn more or get training?

Ask your township fire station (MFSD) about free drills and demonstrations, take a first-aid course with the Myanmar Red Cross, or read the UN-Habitat fire manual — all of them are linked on our Info page, under References.
