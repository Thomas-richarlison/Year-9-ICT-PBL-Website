About Us

A Year 9 ICT project group learning — and teaching — how Myanmar can stop fires before they start.

01 Our purpose

Our Purpose

Our Group wrote this project as a way to understand about fire risks and to reduce as much risks as possible.

Our Mission

To help families in Myanmar understand fire risks and take simple, low-cost steps — clear exits, checked wiring, watched stoves — that stop most fires before they start.

Our Vision

A Myanmar where every household knows what to do before, during and after a fire, so that preventable fires become rare and every fire that does happen stays survivable.
